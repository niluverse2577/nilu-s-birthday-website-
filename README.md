# 🎂 Magical Birthday Celebration Web Application 🎉

A luxurious, interactive, and feature-packed Birthday Celebration Website built with HTML5 Canvas, Web Audio API, and Modern CSS3 Glassmorphism.

---

## ✨ Features Included (Aapke Sare Requirements Ke Saath)

### 1. 📸 Proper 5-6 Photos Gallery (Memories Wall & Lightbox)
- **6 Polaroid Photo Cards** with realistic polaroid tilts, pins, glowing borders, captions, and dates.
- **Batch Upload 6 Photos at Once**: Click "📁 Batch Upload 6 Photos" to select 5-6 photos from your phone or computer.
- **Individual Photo Replacement**: Click any photo card to open the High-Res Lightbox Modal with zoom, Previous/Next navigation, and single photo replacement.
- Pre-loaded with 6 high-resolution aesthetic sample celebration photos!

### 2. 🎵 Custom Song Choice ("Apni Pasand Ka Song")
- **Custom Audio Uploader**: Dedicated button *"🎵 Apni Pasand Ka Song Upload Karein (MP3)"* allows you to select any favorite song or music file from your computer or phone!
- **Built-in Happy Birthday Synthesizer**: Joyful musical melody generated via Web Audio API that plays out-of-the-box (no external internet/broken audio links needed).
- Full player controls: Play/Pause, spinning vinyl record disc, track time seek slider, preset selector, and volume slider.

### 3. 🍰 Interactive 3D Cake Celebration Stage
- **3D Multi-tier Birthday Cake** with cherries, frosting drips, and plate.
- **5 Glowing Candles**: Click individual candles or click **"💨 Blow Candles"** to blow them out with realistic rising smoke puffs and audio whoosh!
- **Knife Cutting Animation**: Click **"🔪 Cut Cake Slice"** to slice the cake with realistic knife movement, cake slice separation, trumpet fanfare music, and massive confetti cannons!

### 4. 🎈 Interactive Floating Balloons
- Real-time 3D styled balloons floating up the screen.
- **Click or Tap to POP**: Realistic pop sound effect, colorful firework burst, and sweet floating celebratory toast messages (e.g. *"You are amazing! 💖"*, *"May all your dreams come true! ✨"*).
- **"Release Balloons" Button**: Spawns a batch of 20 balloons anytime you want.
- Live milestone counter tracking balloons popped!

### 5. ✨ Sparklers (Phuljhadi) & 🎆 Fireworks
- **Golden Sparkler Mouse & Touch Trail**: Move your cursor or swipe your finger anywhere on screen to emit glowing golden sparks like a real Diwali/Birthday sparkler (phuljhadi)!
- **Grand Fireworks System**: Multi-colored exploding rockets with realistic boom sound effects.
- One-click toggles for Sparklers and Fireworks in the top bar.

### 6. 💌 Birthday Wishes Card (Strict Max 200 Words Limit)
- **3D Envelope with Golden Wax Seal**: Click the golden heart seal to unseal the envelope and slide out the glowing greeting card!
- **Heartfelt Hindi + English Pre-filled Wishes**: Pre-loaded with an emotional, poetic wish message (~130 words).
- **Strict Maximum 200 Words Enforced**: Real-time word counter (`XX / 200 words`). If anyone pastes or writes over 200 words, it automatically trims it to 200 and alerts you.
- **One-click Presets**:
  - 🌸 Hindi + English Heartwarming
  - 💖 Deep & Blessed
  - 🎉 Fun, Joy & Success
- **Typewriter Replay**: Re-read the letter with animated typewriter text effect!

### 7. 🎁 Mystery Surprise Gift Box
- Shaking 3D gift box with ribbon that unwraps with confetti and unlocks an "Unlimited Birthday Privilege Pass" voucher!

### 8. ⚙️ Instant Personalization
- Easily change the Birthday Person's Name, Milestone/Age, and Main Banner Photo with instant preview and auto-save in your browser's local storage!
